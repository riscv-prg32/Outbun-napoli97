# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-23

First release.

- OutRun-inspired integer pseudo-3D racer for PRG32 portable cartridges (64 KiB profile, multiplayer).
- Nine timed legs from Napoli to Vietri sul Mare over the real 1997 roads (SS18, SS145 Sorrentina, Massa Lubrense road, SS163 Amalfitana), in 92 sections with the tunnels, the Seiano viaduct, the Punta Scutolo bend, the Nerano and Positano tornanti and the Furore bridge.
- Horizon views per leg: Vesuvius, Monte Faito, Capri, Ischia, Li Galli, Punta Campanella, the Salerno coast; time of day from morning to sunset by palette blending.
- White Fiat 500 hero car; Fiat 126, Citroën Dyane and VW Beetle for the other players and the CPU; Ape, Vespa and SITA coach traffic.
- Route landmarks at their places: Napoli port cranes and Torre del Carmine, the Castellammare shipyard, Castello Giusso, Santa Maria Assunta, the Duomo di Sant'Andrea, the Vietri dome, plus beaches, shrines, lemon stalls, oleanders, pines, palms, agaves and Saracen towers.
- Nine regional ingredients to collect and a layered rosetta panino with a 27-star rating.
- Network play for 2–4 players with a ready lobby, CPU fill and CPU takeover; offline fallback.
- Persistent top-five scores through the firmware score service.
- Six original tracks on eight SID-like procedural voices, plus engine, bell and horn effects.
- Host harness with a route-driving bot, the network scenarios and the draw-call budget; Store bundle validated with the Cartridge Store intake code; smoke-tested in PRG32 QEMU.
