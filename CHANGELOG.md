# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-24 — photo-traced classics

- Redrew the hero car as a Fiat 500 L (1968–72), placed pixel by pixel on a grid measured from rear-view photographs: domed roof with the folded soft-top, framed rear window, slatted grille band, louvred engine lid with chrome handle, plate-light hump over the black two-row plate, tall amber-over-red wing lamps, "500 L" script and thin chrome bumper. The sprite is now 56x48, matching the real car's near-square rear.
- Brake lamps and remote-player markers follow each car's own sprite height.
- The car-select screen names the hero car "FIAT 500 L".
- Redrew the Fiat 126 from a rear-view photograph on the same measured pixel grid: flat roof and near-vertical flanks, wide slanted rear window, flat engine lid with two recessed vertical-slat grilles and the central lock, FIAT and "126" badges, black-housed amber-over-red lamps, wide single-row plate and deep grey plastic bumper. It stays red, so only the player's 500 L is white; it is now 56x48 and its brake lamps follow the new layout.
- Redrew the Citroën Dyane from a rear-view photograph of its 2CV6 sibling on the same measured pixel grid: black canvas roll-top with framed rear window, chrome strip, tall narrow body with split boot lid and handle, separate grey rear wings over thin tyres, badges, square red-over-amber lamps, EU plate and tube bumper. Painted jade green (white and red belong to the 500 L and the 126); now 56x48 with matching brake lamps.
- Redrew the VW Beetle from a rear-view photograph on the same measured pixel grid: domed roof with soft-top, oval rear window with the vents below, rounded engine lid with louvres and handle, wing seams, round lamps on the rear wings, black two-row plate, chrome blade bumper with overriders and twin exhausts. Deep blue as in the reference; now 56x48 with matching brake lamps. All four classics now share the same photo-traced style.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-23

First release.

- OutRun-inspired integer pseudo-3D racer for PRG32 portable cartridges (64 KiB profile, multiplayer).
- Nine timed legs from Napoli to Vietri sul Mare over the real 1997 roads (SS18, SS145 Sorrentina, Massa Lubrense road, SS163 Amalfitana), in 92 sections with the tunnels, the Seiano viaduct, the Punta Scutolo bend, the Nerano and Positano tornanti and the Furore bridge.
- Horizon views per leg: Vesuvius, Monte Faito, Capri, Ischia, Li Galli, Punta Campanella, the Salerno coast; time of day from morning to sunset by palette blending.
- White Fiat 500 hero car; Fiat 126, Citroën Dyane and VW Beetle for the other players and the CPU; Ape, Vespa and SITA coach traffic.
- Route landmarks at their places: Napoli port cranes and Torre del Carmine, the Castellammare shipyard, Castello Giusso, Santa Maria Assunta, the Duomo di Sant'Andrea, the Vietri dome, plus beaches, shrines, lemon stalls, oleanders, pines, palms, agaves and Saracen towers.
- Nine regional ingredients to collect and a layered rosetta panino with a 27-star rating.
- Network play for 2–4 players with a ready lobby, CPU fill and CPU takeover; offline fallback.
- Persistent top-five scores through the firmware score service.
- Six original tracks on eight SID-like procedural voices, plus engine, bell and horn effects.
- Host harness with a route-driving bot, the network scenarios and the draw-call budget; Store bundle validated with the Cartridge Store intake code; smoke-tested in PRG32 QEMU.
